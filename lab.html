WHAT IS MEASURED. WHAT IS MODELED.

The research and the bridge

Measured anatomy: 139,255 FlyWire neurons and 15,091,983 weighted neuron-pair connections; MANC nerve-cord wiring; the detailed Flybody meshes and joint model. Source checksums are recorded in the project.

Simulated physiology: a WebGPU leaky integrate-and-fire implementation derived from webgpu-fly, informed by Shiu et al. It uses simplified neuron parameters and neurotransmitter signs. It is not quantitatively validated against the full research model.

Body interface: FlyWire and MANC are from different specimens, joined here by matching descending-neuron cell-type names. Motor pools are mapped to body actuators with authored gains. The muscle mode has no gait oscillator or root-velocity assist; adhesion remains modeled. Assisted gait uses a scripted tripod pattern and velocity assistance, visibly labeled.

Learning experiment: teaching pulses adjust gains only on existing active KC → MBON connections using a bounded activity-dependent rule. This is an experimental rule, not a validated biological dopamine model. A changed brain is not proof of a learned skill.

Live means live: colors and rates use new GPU readbacks, with a bounded history. The brain and body advance by the same simulated time. Pausing halts both. The app does not replay a neural animation as current activity.

The house and learned daily human activities are not implemented in this corrected research foundation. No synthetic substitute runs in their place.
