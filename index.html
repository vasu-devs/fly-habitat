What is real in this habitat?

Measured anatomy and wiring. The FlyWire v783 graph (139,255 neurons, 15,091,983 directed edges with signed weights) runs a leaky integrate-and-fire model on WebGPU, 1 ms steps, alpha synapses after Shiu et al. 2024. The 3D brain and the pixel matrix show every neuron, colored by live firing rate. The connectome matrix sums the measured weights between populations. The body is TuragaLab flybody's original meshes, joints, 78 actuators and MuJoCo contacts.

Authored senses. Each habitat zone emits an odor plume that decays with distance. Olfactory receptor neurons are split by hemisphere from their measured positions and assigned to five odor channels by index (the prepared data has no glomerulus labels). The antenna facing a source receives more drive. The rendered retina's left and right halves drive the left and right optic lobes.

Real reinforcement learning, two learners. Steering reads left−right activity asymmetries of six populations from the live brain and outputs a turn; it learns by REINFORCE from progress toward the goal. Goal choice is linear Q-learning over interoception, eight population rates and the sensed odor of each candidate. Innate need priorities, two reflexes (righting, escape) and a generation-decaying exploration rate coexist with what is learned. Rewards are algorithmic signals, not simulated dopamine.

Body. Turn and forward commands drive an authored tripod gait with velocity assistance. Navigation assist at 0% means the brain-derived turn alone steers; raising it blends in a coordinate bearing. This is not a measured brain-to-muscle mapping.

Life and inheritance. Hunger, thirst, rest, health, fertility, pollen delivery, eggs and a 240-habitat-second lifespan are simplified rules. On death both learners and the journal are archived and inherited by the next adult (an egg if one exists). Descendants can improve or regress; the lineage panel reports what actually happened.

Interventions. Disconnecting a cell zeroes its incoming and outgoing edges on the GPU; the synaptic-strength slider scales every measured weight. Both persist in the lineage so damaged-brain generations can be compared with intact ones. "Live drive" in the connectome matrix is presynaptic rate × summed weight, an estimate, not a spike-resolved current.

Sources: webgpu-fly · flybody · FlyWire · Shiu et al. model. Everything runs in this tab; no remote inference or hidden training service.
